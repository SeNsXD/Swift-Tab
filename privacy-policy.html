Swift Tab Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Swift Tab is a Windows app by xdNOTLabs that shows a small pill on the taskbar. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

Summary

Swift Tab has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics. We do not collect, sell or share your personal information. Your settings and data stay on your computer.

Data stored on your device

  • Your settings, followed teams and preferences.
  • Items you place on the shelf and the clipboard history shown by the app, which are kept locally.
  • A local log file used for troubleshooting.

None of this is sent to us. Uninstalling the app and deleting its data folder removes it.

Information the app reads on your device

  • The currently playing media (title, artist, artwork) reported by Windows, to show it on the pill.
  • Connected Bluetooth earbuds and their battery level.
  • Clipboard contents, to show the clipboard stash.
  • Files you drop on the pill, for the shelf.

This information is used only on your device to display features you have turned on, and you can turn each feature off in Settings.

Network requests to third-party services

Some features contact third-party services directly from your computer. Depending on which features you enable, the app sends them only the data needed for the request, such as a city name or coordinates, a team name, or a song title and artist:

  • Weather and location search: Open-Meteo (weather, air quality, place search) and OpenStreetMap Nominatim.
  • Sports scores: ESPN and other public score feeds.
  • Lyrics: LRCLIB.
  • Game and update information: Epic Games and GitHub.

These services receive your IP address as with any web request and have their own privacy policies. We do not receive this data.

Local API

The app can run a local server that listens only on your own computer (127.0.0.1), so other programs on your PC can show messages on the pill. It does not accept connections from the internet.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect any information from children.
